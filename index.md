# GoodToGo Support

GoodToGo gets you ready for your next commute: when to leave, the route, the weather, a GoodToGo
alarm, and what to pack, in one calm view. No account, no ads, no analytics.

## Contact

Email **goodtogosupport@gmail.com**. If something isn't working, please include your iPhone model,
your iOS version, and what you expected to happen.

## Privacy

The developer does not collect personal data through GoodToGo. Your plans stay on your iPhone and,
if you turn on iCloud Sync, in your own iCloud. Read the full [Privacy Policy](privacy).

## Common questions

**Why does GoodToGo ask for my location?**
To time your commute from where you are. Your location is sent to Apple Maps and Apple Weather to
answer the request and is not saved by the app.

**Why does it ask for my calendars?**
To find holidays, so weekly plans can skip holidays or run only on holidays. GoodToGo reads your
holiday calendar and does not add or change events.

**Why does it ask for notifications and alarms?**
To remind you before you need to leave, and to ring a GoodToGo alarm if you set one.

**Do I have to allow these?**
No. The app keeps working with less information. You can change your choices at any time in iOS
Settings → Apps → GoodToGo.

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone, in your iPhone's backups, and in the private database of your own iCloud account
if you turn on iCloud Sync. The developer cannot access it.

**How do I delete my data?**
In the app, open the User tab and tap **Delete All Plan Data**. If iCloud Sync is on, this also
deletes your plans from iCloud. Turning iCloud Sync off does not delete the copy already in iCloud;
to remove it, go to iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud, find GoodToGo in the list of apps using
iCloud storage, and delete its data.
